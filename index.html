How I can help

Wellness & prevention: healthy routines, better sleep, movement, balanced eating habits, stress management and sustainable energy — practical, evidence-based advice for everyday life, before small issues become big ones.

Support with long-term health challenges such as Lyme disease, Long Covid, chronic infections or unexplained chronic symptoms: structure, emotional support and practical strategies to rebuild energy and quality of life, working alongside the care of your medical providers.

Together we focus on progress, not perfection — step by step.
